The night after the Midnight Fizz case was cracked, Christina hung a bedsheet across the blanket fort and wrote in huge glitter letters: **THE FORT'S GOT TALENT**.

"Hey guys, Christina here," she whispered to the imaginary camera. "Tonight — five plushies, five talents, zero chill."

Theo bounced out as MC in a bow tie made of popcorn string. "Welcome! Our first act: the one, the only — ANGEL PANDA!"

Angel Panda floated through a slow-motion glow-in-the-dark ribbon dance, landing in a perfect pose. The judges (Snuggle Bug, holding up a crayon-drawn 10) went wild.

Then the lights flickered. A small, chilly breeze swept the stage. The ghost detective glided in, tipping his see-through hat. "Apologies for the interruption. There's been a BACKSTAGE MYSTERY. The teapot has vanished."

A gasp. The bedtime tea finale was supposed to be the closing act!

Boo Bear was already on the case, magnifying glass out. "Follow the drips." They followed the tiniest trail of tea across the floor — right under the pillow pile, where the teapot was hiding, trembling.

"Stage fright," whispered Christina, scooting the teapot close. "Even teapots get nervous." Hazza gave it a warm hug with both paws, and the teapot puffed bravely. Mystery solved in sixty seconds — a new record.

The show roared on: DJ Malik's record-scratch lullaby remix, Hazza's dramatic sneeze song (a standing ovation AND a sneeze forecast alert), and Boo Bear's detective comedy act. Then the Midnight Charms closed the night with a song Liam wrote on the spot — *about the talent show itself*, with a line contributed by every single plushie.

For the grand finale, Finn declared a British vs. American slang battle. "You cheeky little biscuit!" shouted Christina. "Well, you absolute goofball cookie!" shot back Theo. Everyone agreed the kindest insult wins — and they were ALL winners.

Then, finally, the teapot took center stage, brave and shiny, and poured the most calming Healing Bites bedtime tea — chamomile, honey, and a whisper of magic sparkle. The whole crew sipped together, warm and sleepy.

"Goodnight, talent show. Goodnight, slang battle. Goodnight, brave teapot." 🍵✨🧸
