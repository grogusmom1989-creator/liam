"Hey guys, Christina here," Christina said into the flashlight microphone, because everything good deserves to be filmed. "Welcome to PLUSHIE NEWSROOM — LIVE from the blanket fort. All the news that's cozy to print."

Angel Panda took the anchor desk (a pillow). Snuggle Bug adjusted his tie — which was a ribbon. Boo Bear held up cue cards. DJ Malik dropped the theme music. Hazza was the weather… well, the snack-weather reporter. Close enough.

"Breaking news," Angel Panda announced in her very serious anchor voice. "SIX socks have vanished from the laundry basket. The ghost detective has declared it a cold case."

The ghost detective floated in wearing a tiny fedora. "No footprints. No crumbs. Just… single socks. This one goes deep."

The Midnight Charms formed the investigation team. Liam questioned the dryer (no comment). Theo dusted for lint. Kai interviewed the matching pairs — all innocent. Rex sketched a suspect board with yarn and sticky notes. Finn sat in the laundry basket with a flashlight, waiting for the thief to strike again.

Then came the Daily Sneeze Forecast. "Snuggle Bug reports a 90% chance of dramatic sneezes before bedtime," Christina announced, and right on cue — "ACHOO!" — Snuggle Bug sneezed so hard his ribbon tie flew off. The whole fort exploded in giggles. The ghost detective marked it: *CASE CLOSED… the giggles, not the socks.*

Finn gasped from the basket. "FOUND THEM!" He held up the missing socks — all six, balled into one giant sock ball, plus a very guilty-looking Boo Bear. "Boo Bear made a Sock Snowman in July," Christina laughed, hugging him. "Case REALLY closed!"

As a final broadcast segment, the plush crew gathered close for a bedtime story read-aloud — soft ASMR voices, whispered "the end," and magic Healing Bites bedtime tea for everyone. Theo strummed the softest chord. The ghost detective tipped his tiny fedora goodnight.

"That's our Newsroom, our Sneeze Forecast, and one sock-y mystery solved," Christina whispered to the camera. "Goodnight, everybody." 🌙📺🧦
