"Hey guys, Christina here," Christina grinned into the imaginary camera, because everything good deserves to be filmed. "Tonight's mission: a WORLD SNACK TOUR — in the fort. With tiny tasting spoons. And judges."

The plush crew lined up like the world's softest tasting panel. Angel Panda adjusted her judge sash. Snuggle Bug held a notepad. DJ Malik spun a little drumroll on his turntable. Boo Bear took the snacks VERY seriously. Hazza just wanted the chocolate one.

First stop: tiny purple ube bites — sweet, soft, gone in one sniff. Then mini honey puffs — Snuggle Bug's whole face glowed. Then churro curls dipped in warm chocolate — and Christina declared a three-way tie because, quote, "choosing one winner would be emotionally devastating."

But just as everyone applauded, a shivery little *plink… plink… plink* drifted from the corner of the fort. The ghost detective floated in, holding a dusty music box. "Every night at bedtime," he whispered, "this box plays a tune NO ONE winds up. Haunted, I say. Haunted!"

Finn of the Midnight Charms gasped. Theo dimmed the lamp. Kai grabbed his guitar — just in case the ghost needed backup.

Christina lifted the lid slowly… and the sweetest, softest lullaby tinkled out. Not spooky at all. Just sleepy. Just sweet. "Mystery solved," Christina whispered. "This music box wasn't haunted — it was just humming babies and bands to sleep."

The whole fort melted. Liam played a gentle harmony alongside the tinkling lullaby while Rex scribbled lyrics on a napkin ("Plink, plink, dream away…"). Angel Panda tucked everyone in, Snuggle Bug was already snoring, and the ghost detective floated off to bed humming the tune.

Christina brewed one last cup of magic Healing Bites bedtime tea, waved sleepily at the camera, and whispered: "That's our world snack tour, our haunted-ish music box, and one perfect lullaby. Goodnight, everybody." 🌙✨🧸
