"Hey guys, Christina here," Christina whispered from under her JoJo Siwa comforter, flashlight glowing. "Welcome to the Midnight Diner. Tonight's special: cozy snacks… and one tiny cold case."

The diner was open for business. Angel Panda was the waiter — with a notepad he kept holding upside down. Hazza ordered "one milkshake," but with the loud dramatic delivery of an Uncle Louie sketch: "WAIT-ah — no, YES-ah — the SHAKE!" DJ Malik ordered fries, then promptly changed his mind six times. Boo Bear just pointed at everything and said, "That one. And that one. And that one."

"Order confusion: complete!" Christina laughed, tripping over the rug on her way to deliver the milkshake — Clumsy Cutie Chronicle number one — and catching it like a champ. "No limits, just fun," she grinned.

But wait. The ghost detective floated in, tiny fedora on, extra-serious. "Detectives' report," he said. "Tonight's cold case: the Vanishing Comforter. Someone's Super Mario nap blanket is missing. Repeat. MISSING."

The plush crew gasped. Snuggle Bug led the investigation, checking behind the couch cushions (crumb evidence), under the bed (dust-bunny witnesses), and inside the blanket fort (suspiciously lumpy corners). Theo drummed the theme music. Kai held a tiny magnifying glass.

Then — a squeak. From the lumpy corner of the fort. Out tumbled three tiny Tigger plushies, four Grogu plushies, and one very tangled Super Mario blanket. "THEY were having their own midnight diner," Christina whispered, gathering all the babies into one big squishy hug. "Cold case: SOLVED. The thieves were adorable."

For the grand finale, everyone shared warm mugs of magic Healing Bites bedtime tea while the Midnight Charms sang the Diner Goodnight Lullaby — low, soft, sing-along harmonies, Liam's guitar gentle as a heartbeat. The ghost detective tipped his fedora.

"That's our diner night, our Louie-style ordering, and one blanket mystery solved," Christina yawned into the camera. "Goodnight, everybody." 🌙🍩🧸
