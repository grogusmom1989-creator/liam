"Hey guys, Christina here," Christina said into her flashlight microphone, walking into the blanket fort — and straight into a pillow wall. *Bonk.* "And that, friends, is Clumsy Cutie Chronicle number one!"

Tonight was Snack Sunday in the Blanket Fort Cinema. Angel Panda rolled out the snack cart. Boo Bear wore a chef hat three sizes too big. On the cart: Filipino banana chips and polvoron, Greek loukoumades (honey puffs), and Spanish churros with thick chocolate. Snuggle Bug's eyes went starry.

Christina tripped over the snack cart — Chronicle number two — and caught the churro plate with her foot like a soccer star. "SAVE!" yelled Theo. The whole crew cheered. She dropped a loukoumades on her own toe — Chronicle number three — and took a dramatic bow anyway. "No limits, just fun," she grinned, her motto glowing on the fort wall.

Tasting time! The Midnight Charms sang a Flavor-Rank Jingle for each bite. Banana chips: "Crunchy, golden, fun-time crunch!" Polvoron: "Melty, powdery, sneeze-with-love!" Liam strummed as Kai harmonized, Rex drummed on a popcorn bowl, and Finn conducted with a churro like a maestro's baton.

Then the ghost detective floated in, tiny fedora tipped. "Detectives' report: the chocolate-dipped churros… are missing. Repeat. MISSING." Blank stares. Then — giggles from behind the popcorn bowl. Snuggle Bug's face was 100% chocolate.

"Case of the Vanishing Churros: SOLVED by the crumbs on your cheeks, sir!" Christina laughed, scooping him into a hug. "We love a honest thief."

As the grand finale, the Midnight Charms sang the Blanket Fort Goodnight Jingle while everyone sipped magic Healing Bites bedtime tea. Theo strummed the softest chord. The ghost detective tipped his fedora goodnight.

"That's our clumsy chronicles, our snack-tasting, and one churro mystery solved," Christina whispered to the camera. "Goodnight, everybody." 🌙🍫🧸
