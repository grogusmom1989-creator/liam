"Hey guys, Christina here," Christina whispered into the camcorder, careful not to wake the sleeping street. The porch was a moonlit wonderland: paper stars taped to the railing, twinkling fairy lights, and a long table covered in tiny teacups — one for every single friend.

The ghost detective floated in wearing his fanciest bow tie. "Welcome," he said in his echo-y voice, "to the first-ever Friendly Ghost Tea Party. Tonight, we don't solve mysteries. Tonight, we sip." He poured moonbeam tea that sparkled silver in every cup.

First came the Moonlit Healing Hands moment. Christina held her palms up to the moon and gathered its glow like she was scooping starlight. "Okay, crew," she said softly, "little healing hugs going out." She gave each teacup a tiny warm blessing — angel panda's cup got courage, snuggle bug's got giggles, Hazza's got extra-strong sneeze-control (just in case), DJ Malik's got rhythm, and boo bear's got the coziest of cozy. The Midnight Charms held their cups like they were holding moonlight itself: Liam strummed a soft thank-you chord, Theo curled up backstage-style with his teacup on his tummy, Kai sighed dreamily, Rex went quiet and gentle for once, and Finn — scene-stealer — balanced his cup on his nose and earned a round of clapping from everyone.

Then the music. Liam's guitar played the slowest, softest version of their newest song, and Kai's voice floated out over the porch like a warm blanket. The plush crew leaned on each other, eyes heavy. The ghost detective sipped his tea and sighed a happy ghosty sigh. "Case closed," he whispered. "The mystery of the perfect night... solved. It was friendship."

Christina curled into the middle of the plush pile, teacup warm in her hands. "Goodnight, moon," she whispered. "Goodnight, tea party." And under the porch light, surrounded by everyone she loves, she drifted off to the sound of a lullaby and one very tiny, very happy ghostly slurp of tea. 🌙🍵🧸
