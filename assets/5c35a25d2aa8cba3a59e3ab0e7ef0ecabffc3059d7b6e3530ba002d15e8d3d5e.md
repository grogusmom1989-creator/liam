"Hey guys, Christina here," Christina whispered into the imaginary camera, pulling the blanket over her head like a news tent. "Tonight, we re-open the FORT'S OLDEST COLD CASE."

The plush crew sat in a circle, solemn as popcorn judges. Angel Panda pinned on a tiny detective badge made from a sticker. The ghost detective drifted in, fog and all, carrying a dusty little box labeled *CASE #001: THE MISSING MIDNIGHT SNACK*.

"It was the night of the very first blanket fort," the ghost detective whispered. "A cookie vanished without a trace. For years, no one knew where it went."

Boo Bear gasped. "Wait. I remember this case!" He reached under the cushion and pulled out — a dusty crumb trail leading to the corner where Snuggle Bug sat, cheeks very, very round.

Snuggle Bug sighed in the tiniest, softest ASMR voice. "I saved it. For the perfect bedtime moment. And tonight... is the moment." She shared the cookie crumble among all five plushies, and the case was closed with giggles and crumbs.

To celebrate the solved case, Christina threw a surprise **Family Zip Fizz party** — tiny paper cups of fizzy blue and pink Zip Fizz for everyone. DJ Malik dropped a toast beat on his mini turntable, and the whole crew clinked cups. (Even the ghost detective got a sip. "Effervescent," he said, floating a little higher.)

Then Theo dimmed the lamp, and the real bedtime began: the plush crew's first-ever **read-aloud**. Each plush took a page of the fort's storybook in a soft, slow, whispery voice — Angel Panda on chapter one, Hazza on chapter two, all the way down to Boo Bear's sleepy ending. Liam of the Midnight Charms played gentle guitar underneath, and Christina's reiki healing light — soft, golden, calm — shimmered over the whole fort.

By the last page, everyone was dozing: cookie crumbs, Zip Fizz fizz, one closed case, and five plushies tucked under the blanket.

"Goodnight, cold case. Goodnight, read-aloud. Goodnight, healing light." 🌙✨🧸
